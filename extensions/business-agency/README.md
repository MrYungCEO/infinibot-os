# Business & Agency (first-party plugin)

HQ, Command, Clients, Sales Pipeline, Register, Leads & Funnels and Business
settings in one sidebar section, plus the topbar agency selector, the business
roster agents, business agent tools and the business schedules.

It is off on a fresh install. An install that already has business data keeps
it on. Turning it off never deletes data.

Full design, the list of what moved and what stayed core, the seam, the
migration and the tests are in [docs/plugins/BUSINESS-AGENCY.md](../../docs/plugins/BUSINESS-AGENCY.md).
