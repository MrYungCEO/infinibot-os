## How the limits work

- **Seat:** one person (employee or contractor) who develops, administers, supports or operates a licensed deployment. Your product's end users and automated agents are not seats.
- **Organization:** a legal entity, or a separately administered deployment or workspace, that you run a licensed deployment for. For Agency and Enterprise, each client you run, resell or administer a separate deployment for counts. End customers who only use your multi-tenant product do not count.
- **Annual Gross Revenue:** the total revenue of you and your affiliates in your last fiscal year or any trailing 12 months, whichever is greater (investment, loans and grants excluded). You self-certify it when you buy or renew. If you go above any limit, upgrade within 30 days.
- **White-label** (Agency and Enterprise): you may replace InfiniBot branding in your product's user interface. You must keep the copyright and license notices in the source and in an "about" or "open-source licenses" screen or file. No trademark license is included.

## Billing

- Every tier is a recurring subscription through Whop and **renews automatically** at the end of each period until you cancel in Whop or by email to https://infinibotos.com. Cancelling stops the next renewal; your license runs to the end of the paid period.
- Agency and Enterprise bill every 180 and 60 days because our payment processor caps a single recurring card charge below $2,500 for this account. The annual economics shown above are approximate.
- If a renewal payment fails, the license key stays valid for a 14-day grace period. After expiry or cancellation you have a 30-day wind-down period for existing customers (see Section 11 of the Agreement).
- Fees are non-refundable once your license key is issued, except for duplicate or mistaken charges, non-delivery of the key within 5 business days, or where law requires (Section 10 of the Agreement).
