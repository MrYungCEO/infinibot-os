Droid Swarm

Run a rack of Android phones from one console: devices, accounts, workflows and egress routes.

Who it's for

Social media operators who run real accounts on real phones and want one console with human checkpoints.

What it does

  • Enrol phones over USB or wireless debugging, watch and drive the live screen
  • Assign accounts to phones; secrets never enter the database
  • Scheduled workflows, one egress route per phone and a full audit log

InfiniBot (first-party)

Controls phones on your own network (adb over your LAN or tailnet) and spawns local tools (adb, Tailscale, scrcpy). Available on self-hosted InfiniBot; a cloud bridge to your phones does not exist yet.

How to start

  1. Install adb on the computer running InfiniBot, then enable the plugin.
  2. Enrol a phone with the companion APK or wireless debugging and pin it to your tailnet.
  3. Import an account, bind it to a phone and approve your first post from Content Studio.

What you need

  • Self-hosted InfiniBot (not available on InfiniBot Cloud)
  • One or more Android phones and adb on the host computer
  • Optional: Tailscale, Fly.io and Cloudflare for remote phones and egress

Responsible use

Automating social accounts can break a platform's terms of service and can get accounts restricted or banned. Use your own accounts, respect each platform's rules and limits, and keep a human approval on every post. The course's Platform Rules lesson covers the real risks.

Watch: Platform Rules, Real Risk and Responsible Use

Questions

Does it work on InfiniBot Cloud?

No. It controls phones on your own network with adb, so it needs a self-hosted InfiniBot.

Will it get my accounts banned?

It can if you break a platform's rules. Droid Swarm adds limits and approval steps, but you are responsible for how accounts are used.
