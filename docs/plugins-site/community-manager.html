Discord Community Manager

Run your Discord with InfiniBot: welcome and verification, intake, tickets and moderation with approvals.

Included with InfiniBot

Who it's for

Community owners and coaches running a Discord server.

What it does

  • Welcome, verification and an intake questionnaire with lead scoring
  • Ticket threads and scheduled posts
  • Moderation with an approval gate for bans, mass DMs and @everyone
Price
Included with InfiniBot
Publisher
InfiniBot (built in)

How to start

  1. Connect your Discord bot to InfiniBot.
  2. Run the guided setup wizard on the /community dashboard.
  3. Review approvals before any ban, mass DM or @everyone goes out.

What you need

  • A Discord server and a Discord bot
  • Included with InfiniBot, not sold separately: check that your InfiniBot shows the Community page
