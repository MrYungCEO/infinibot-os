Who it's for

TradeLocker traders who want their journal, scanner and research next to their agents.

What it does

  • Trading terminal, journal, scanner, signals and economic calendar
  • Analyst agent with live trade, chart and journal panels in chat
  • Read-only by default; orders need a separate permission and a typed confirmation

How to start

  1. Install Infinitrade from the marketplace.
  2. Connect your TradeLocker account (read-only by default).
