Who it's for

Clinicians, medical students and researchers.

What it does

  • Feed of papers, trials and drug-safety notices from public sources
  • Ask a question, get a cited briefing
  • Your own tables for conditions, drugs, cases and protocols

Research uses public sources (PubMed, Europe PMC, ClinicalTrials.gov, openFDA, bioRxiv) and your workspace's own AI model. Clinic data stays on your workspace's private machine; InfiniBot Cloud is not a HIPAA-covered service.

How to start

  1. Install OmniClinic from the marketplace.
  2. Pick your specialties for the intelligence feed.
  3. Ask the research engine a question and save what matters to your databases.
