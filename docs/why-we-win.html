01 The OS race

Everyone builds a chatbot. InfiniBot owns the OS layer

Everyone talks about models and chat boxes. But if a smart brain can not see your devices, use your tools, or be watched — it is just a demo. The winner is the layer that turns any smart brain into real work — on every device you own.

  1. Model era

    Top smart brains pass each other every few months. The gaps get smaller. If you bet on one, you lose when the next one comes out.

  2. Chatbot era

    A chat window gives ideas. It does not do the work. It forgets your context, can not reach your other devices, and makes you connect all the tools yourself.

  3. OS era

    Operating systems outlive the apps below them. They own how work gets done. InfiniBot is that layer for AI — works with any smart brain, connects your devices, and actually does the work.
