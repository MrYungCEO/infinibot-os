## Why this policy exists

People need to be able to tell whether they are using official InfiniBot
software or InfiniBot Cloud, or something else. The policy is meant to be
permissive for the community and to prevent confusion. It is not meant to
stop you from talking about InfiniBot or building on it.

## Uses you don't need to ask about

- **Referring to InfiniBot** in articles, videos, talks, reviews, tutorials,
  books and social posts.
- **Accurate nominative statements** about your own product or project, such
  as:
  - "built on InfiniBot"
  - "based on InfiniBot"
  - "compatible with InfiniBot"
  - "for InfiniBot" (for plugins, skills, extensions and packs)
  - "InfiniBot integration"
- **Unmodified distribution.** Redistributing unmodified official releases
  under the original name, with the license and notices intact.
- **Community groups** (meetups, forums, chat servers) that use the name to
  describe their topic, as long as they don't look official. For example,
  "Berlin InfiniBot Users" is fine; "InfiniBot Official Berlin" is not.

When you use the Marks this way:

- use the name as written ("InfiniBot") and don't alter the logo;
- make your own brand the most prominent one on your product;
- don't imply that we sponsor, endorse or certify you unless we have agreed
  to it in writing.

## Uses that need our written permission

- **Forks and modified versions must rebrand.** If you distribute a modified
  version, or offer it as a service, give it a different name and logo that
  are not confusingly similar to InfiniBot. You may still say it is "based on
  InfiniBot".
- Using "InfiniBot" or the logo in the **name of a product, company, service,
  domain name or app-store listing**.
- Using the words **"Official", "Cloud", "Enterprise", "Certified"** or
  similar next to "InfiniBot", or anything suggesting you are InfiniBot
  Cloud.
- Using the Marks on **merchandise** you sell.
- Using the tagline **"the AI OS"** together with "InfiniBot", or in a way that
  suggests your product is InfiniBot.
- Using the Marks in **advertising keywords** or in any way likely to confuse
  people about who made the product.

Commercial and OEM licensees can get a trademark license as part of their
agreement. See [`COMMERCIAL.md`](COMMERCIAL.md).

## Never allowed

- Using the Marks for software that contains malware, or that has been
  changed to collect data without users' clear consent.
- Using the Marks in a way that is false, misleading or disparaging.
- Registering confusingly similar trademarks, domain names or social handles.
